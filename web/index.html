The Earth beneath the numbers

The World Magnetic Model describes the large-scale field generated within Earth. Local rock, metal, nearby electronics and magnetic storms can change what a physical compass observes. This app does not access your device’s compass or position.

The standard degree-12 WMM2025 model is bundled here. Dates outside 2025–2029 are rejected; the next model must be installed before using later dates.

Near the magnetic poles

NOAA defines a blackout zone when horizontal field strength is below 2,000 nT, and a caution zone from 2,000 to 6,000 nT. The app flags these areas. Bearing conversion is unavailable in a blackout zone or exactly at a geographic pole.

The displayed declination uncertainty is NOAA’s one-standard-deviation model estimate at Earth’s surface. It does not cover every local disturbance or establish reliable navigation.

Your inputs stay here

Coordinates, dates and bearings are calculated locally. No geocoding service or magnetic API receives them, and this app does not save them automatically. Download JSON or CSV if you want a copy.

Height is metres above the WGS84 ellipsoid; no geoid correction is applied. Accepted heights are −1 km to 850 km. Real model accuracy also depends on height and geomagnetic conditions.
